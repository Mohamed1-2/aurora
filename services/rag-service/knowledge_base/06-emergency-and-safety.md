# Emergency and Safety

## Coolant over-temperature

A coolant_temp_c above 103 C is grounds to reduce load: drop to Eco mode, slow down and open windows to reduce HVAC load. Above 110 C, pull over as soon as it is safe, switch off the vehicle and wait for the temperature to fall before continuing. Do not open the coolant reservoir while hot.

## Battery over-temperature

If battery_temp_c exceeds 42 C, DCFC fast charging is not recommended until temperature falls, because heat accelerates pack ageing and charging efficiency drops sharply when the battery is hot. Reduce load and let the thermal system cool the pack.

## Tyre blowout risk

A rapid single-corner pressure drop (more than 25 kPa over a minute) while driving indicates a puncture. Hold steering steady, ease off the accelerator gently, apply no hard braking, and move to hard shoulder once speed is low.

## Load limits and towing

Do not tow with the AURORA. Roof loading up to 80 kg is permitted. Payload reduces range and increases brake and tyre temperatures.

## Connective features

In an incident, eCall automatically notifies emergency services with the GPS position from the telemetry stream. The assistant can also call roadside assistance for tyre changes, lockouts and flat-battery events.
