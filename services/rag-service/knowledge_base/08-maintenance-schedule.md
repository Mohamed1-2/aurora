# Maintenance Schedule

## Scheduled service intervals

The AURORA is serviced every 24 months or 30,000 km, whichever comes first. Service includes: high-voltage system inspection, coolant level and condition, brake fluid (replace every 3 years), cabin air filter (replace every 2 years), 12 V auxiliary battery health check, and tyre rotation every 10,000 km.

## Brake system

Regen braking greatly reduces physical brake wear, but pads should be inspected at each service for oxidation and corrosion, common in low-usage brake systems. Rotors are replaced based on measured thickness, not mileage.

## Tyres

Check pressure monthly and before long trips, inspect tread at quarterly interval, rotate every 10,000 km. Tyre age matters: replace tyres older than 6 years regardless of tread.

## 12 V battery

The auxiliary 12 V battery powers computers, locks, telematics and the infotainment while the contactor is open. If the vehicle reports a 12 V system fault or sluggish accessory behaviour, the 12 V battery may need replacement (typical life 3-5 years).

## Cabin filter and AC

Cabin air filter every 2 years or sooner in dusty or pollen-heavy environments. AC refrigerant is sealed; only service if cooling performance drops.
