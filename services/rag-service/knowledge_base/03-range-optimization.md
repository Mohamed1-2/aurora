# Range Optimization

## Driving style

Eco mode combined with anticipation (smooth inputs, early release of the accelerator to maximise regen) raises usable range by up to 18% versus Sport mode with aggressive braking. Prefer gentle acceleration: peak torque is rarely needed for normal traffic progress.

## Speed matters most

Aerodynamic drag dominates above 90 km/h. At 120 km/h the vehicle uses roughly 30% more energy per kilometre than at 100 km/h. Range is maximised by holding a steady 100-110 km/h on motorways rather than cycling between 120 and 80.

## Climate control

Cabin HVAC can account for 10-15% of energy draw in extreme weather. Pre-conditioning while plugged in uses grid power rather than battery: set a departure schedule in the app to defrost and heat the cabin before unplugging.

## Tyres

Under-inflated tyres increase rolling resistance. Each 20 kPa below the 240 kPa nominal raises consumption about 1%. Cold weather drops tyre pressure naturally; check and re-inflate as pressure drops, and set tyre pressures to the values printed on the driver's door placard.

## Terrain

Recover energy with regen on the downhill of every climb. Set the sat-nav route even for familiar trips so the vehicle can optimise battery state-of-charge across the whole route. Uphill-highway combinations are the most demanding; plan charging stops accordingly when below 25% SOC.
