# Tyres and Wheels

## Nominal pressures

Front and rear tyres are inflated to a nominal 240 kPa at 20 C. Correct pressures are printed on the driver's door placard. Pressures rise with speed (roughly +1.5 kPa per 100 km/h) and with tyre temperature; small day-to-day variation is normal.

## Low pressure and safety

If live tyre pressure drops below 210 kPa the vehicle should advise the driver to inflate. Below 200 kPa the tyre-monitoring system illuminates a warning and the assistant must recommend verifying all four corners, comparing the reported values, and inspecting for leaks or punctures before further highway driving. Below 170 kPa the tyre is unsafe for continued driving and the driver should slow, assess and stop to change the wheel or inflate.

## Cold weather

Each 10 C drop in ambient temperature reduces tyre pressure by approximately 10-14 kPa. A cold-morning drop into the 200-215 kPa band is expected and is not a fault; it resolves with inflation and warmer temperatures.

## Quarterly inspection

Inspect tread depth (minimum 1.6 mm legal, 3 mm recommended) and look for uneven wear, which indicates alignment or suspension issues. Rotate tyres every 10,000 km per the maintenance schedule.
