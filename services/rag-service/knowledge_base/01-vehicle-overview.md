# AURORA Platform Overview

## Powertrain

The AURORA is a series-hybrid electric vehicle. A nominal 82 kWh high-voltage lithium-ion battery drives a 165 kW rear traction motor. A 92 kW range-extender engine acts purely as a generator: it never drives the wheels directly, and only runs to top up the battery when state of charge is critically low (below 18%) or when Sport mode demands sustained high power.

## Driving modes

Three drive modes are available from the center stack. Eco mode limits acceleration torque and maximises regenerative braking recovery, optimising range. Normal mode balances performance and efficiency with 60 kW of max regenerative braking. Sport mode raises motor power limits, tightens the throttle map and relaxes battery temperature limits at the cost of higher energy consumption and drivetrain heat.

## Regen braking

Release the accelerator and the motor reverses into a generator, capturing kinetic energy back into the battery. The instrument cluster shows negative motor power (kW) during regen. Full stop from speed uses blended hydraulic brakes only below 8 km/h. Aggressive but anticipated deceleration recovers the most energy.

## Connectivity

The vehicle is connected to the AURORA cloud platform over a secure TLS telemetry link at 10 Hz. The in-vehicle AI assistant uses this live stream, together with this knowledge base, to answer driver questions in real time. No raw personal data leaves the vehicle unless it is anonymised.
