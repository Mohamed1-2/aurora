# Owners' Manual: Extended Reference

## Warranty

The high-voltage battery is warrantied for 8 years or 160,000 km with at least 70% of original usable capacity retained. The rest of the vehicle carries a 5-year / 100,000 km factory warranty. Maintenance performed at any certified workshop does not void the warranty, but modifications to powertrain software or components do.

## Driver assistance systems

Standard ADAS includes adaptive cruise control, lane keeping, automatic emergency braking and blind-spot monitoring. Adaptive cruise can be used between 0 and 140 km/h. The systems are driver aids, not autonomous driving; the driver must remain attentive with hands on the wheel.

## Software updates

The vehicle receives over-the-air updates for telematics, UI and driver-assistance logic. High-voltage or safety software is updated at a certified workshop. The assistant runs a versioned prompt policy (aurora-prompt) so behaviour changes are tracked and evaluated before release.

## Climate and cabin

The heat-pump HVAC is most efficient above 5 C; below that the resistive booster assists. Auto mode manages humidity and defogging automatically. Cabin humidity is reported so the assistant can advise on demisting before the driver asks.

## Connectivity and data

The vehicle sends telemetry (speed, SOC, temperatures, DTCs, tyre pressures, GPS) to AURORA at 10 Hz for assistant grounding, drift monitoring and evaluation. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and is not sold.
