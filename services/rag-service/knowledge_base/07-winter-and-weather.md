# Winter and Weather Driving

## Cold-weather range

Cold weather reduces range three ways: denser air increases aero drag, thermal management warms the battery (a drag on efficiency), and cabin heating draws from the pack. Expect range to drop 15-30% below -5 C. Pre-conditioning while plugged in shifts heating load to grid power.

## Snow and ice

Regen re-engagement in slippery conditions is managed by the stability controller. Use winter tyres below 7 C ambient. In deep snow, reduce regen aggressiveness if available in the menu, and start in Eco mode for softer torque.

## Charging in the cold

The battery charges more slowly below 0 C until the pack warms. Do not DCFC repeatedly from very low SOC in deep cold; plan a buffer and rely on AC charging overnight which keeps the pack running warmer and conditioning on schedule.

## Battery temperature management

The vehicle pre-heats the battery when plugged in and a departure or charging window is set. A warm pack charges faster, never exceeds the operating band, and loses less capacity over its life.
